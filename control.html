Energy Bodies — Crowd

Energy Bodies reads the whole room at once — everyone in frame gets their own living Energy Body on the display.

This panel doesn't set anyone's values by hand. It just starts and stops the camera, and shows what it's currently reading from the group.

Participants can enter or leave the frame at any time. No identifiable personal data is stored.

Session Guide

Press Start to begin tracking everyone in frame — each person's movement becomes their own blob of light on the display. Press Print to take home an image of the room's collective energy.
